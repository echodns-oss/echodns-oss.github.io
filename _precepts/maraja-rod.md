item: Fishing Rod

Casts penetrate the Border Immaterial, allowing the user of this rod to catch and entreat water familiars, even when fishing in stagnant streams or small puddles.

Maraja is exceptionally skilled at catching fish. This is not a special property unique to the Precept, however.
